## About Me

I'm pursuing a dual academic path: **B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering at JG University** and **BS in Data Science at IIT Madras**.

My work sits at the intersection of computer science, data, and intelligent systems. I'm especially drawn to **Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, and Computer Vision**.

Beyond coursework, I teach and mentor, and I enjoy breaking complex ideas into simple, clear explanations.
